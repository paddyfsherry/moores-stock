    Wine list generator

    Build a printable price list straight from the wine list — duty-paid or in-bond (list price less duty), with a customer’s discount applied if you pick one. Save any set-up as a template so it can be reprinted in two clicks, always with today’s prices and stock.

    Delivery notes

    Every delivery note comes from an invoice — open any invoice or draft (from Sales or Reports) and press Delivery note. The customer, address and lines fill in; untick lines for a split delivery. Prices never print. For samples, replacements or gifts, raise a zero-priced draft invoice first — that way every departure of stock has a document and a movement behind it.
